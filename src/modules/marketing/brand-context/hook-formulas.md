# Jaxy Hook Formulas (video)

*Proven, currently-trending opening lines for short-form. The AI writes each
video's hook in ONE of these styles, adapted to the audience, the pillar, and
the featured frame. **Keep this fresh** — when a new format is working, add it
here and retire stale ones; it flows into every generated video and idea with
no redeploy.*

## How to use a formula

- Pick the formula that fits the clip + pillar, then fill the [brackets] from the
  audience profile / featured product.
- The BURNED on-screen hook is a tight ≤60-char adaptation of the formula (front
  half of the line). For talking-head clips that keep their audio, the full
  formula is also the spoken opener.
- Specific > generic. Never invent a product that isn't featured.

## Trending formulas (rotate — don't reuse the same one every time)

1. **Identity call-out** — name the person it's for so they stop scrolling.
   - "This video is for my [identity] girlies…" (e.g. no-makeup-makeup, low-maintenance, always-cold)
   - On-screen: "for my no-makeup girlies 👇"

2. **Obsessed / discovery** (TikTok-Shop-native) — genuine excitement.
   - "These sunglasses I got off TikTok Shop — I'm literally obsessed…"
   - On-screen: "obsessed with these 🕶️"

3. **Numbered reasons** — fast, listicle energy, easy to finish.
   - "Number one, they protect your eyes. Number two, they're so cute…"
   - On-screen: "reasons these are worth it →"

4. **Relatable problem → solution** — a moment they've lived, then the fix.
   - "You know when the sun's too bright and you start squinting? Yeah — these fix that."
   - On-screen: "when the sun's too bright ☀️"

5. **"It's giving" aesthetic** — name the vibe/aesthetic they're buying.
   - "If you don't really wear makeup, these are THE look. It's giving smart, educated baddie."
   - On-screen: "it's giving educated baddie"

6. **Favorite / haul** — casual, "I got them in [color]".
   - "I got them in [color] — I think this might be my favorite pair."
   - On-screen: "[color] might be my favorite"

7. **POV** — put them in the scene.
   - "POV: [the moment / the feeling with the glasses]"
   - On-screen: "POV: you found your shape"

8. **Anti-gatekeep** — insider recommendation, low-key flex.
   - "Not gatekeeping these anymore…"
   - On-screen: "not gatekeeping these"

## Rules

- Match the formula's energy to the audience (friend-with-taste, playful, never salesy).
- Ladder back to the brand: distinctive, guilt-free, "More frames. Less shame."
- Keep the burned on-screen version SHORT (≤60 chars) so it reads in the first 2s.
