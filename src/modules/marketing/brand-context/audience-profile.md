# Jaxy Audience Profile (video)

*The person on the other side of the screen. Every generated video's hook,
caption, and script should be written to THIS person — not to "sunglasses
buyers" in general. Specificity is what stops the scroll. Edit freely as we
learn who's actually converting.*

## Who they are

- **18–45, skews Gen Z / younger millennial, mostly women (but unisex appeal).** US, urban and suburban.
- Budget-conscious but **style-first**. Shops Zara, H&M, the Target style section, Amazon, boutiques.
- **Social-media native** — lives on TikTok, Instagram, Pinterest. Watches with the sound off half the time.
- Wants to own **multiple pairs** for different moods and outfits — glasses as an accessory you collect, not an investment you protect.

## What they want (desired outcomes)

- To look put-together and a little bit *different* — frames that start a conversation, not blend in.
- To feel confident and expressive without spending designer money.
- An easy, low-stakes yes: "cute, cheap, buy two."

## What frustrates them (the enemy)

- Having to choose between **boring-but-affordable** ($2–15 generic) and **cool-but-$150+** designer.
- Expensive glasses they're scared to lose, scratch, or sit on.
- Feeling like every affordable brand looks the same.

## Fears & objections (answer these in content)

- "Will they look cheap in person?" → show them on real faces, in real light, in motion.
- "$25 sunglasses, are they junk?" → distinctive design + real detail reads as quality; let the look do the arguing.
- "Do these suit my face?" → variety of shapes on variety of people; help them self-select.

## Buying triggers

- **Seeing someone cool wearing them** (the #1 trigger — creator/UGC, real fits).
- Outfit completion, a vibe, a mood, a season change.
- Impulse: low price + "two of these, please" makes it a guilt-free add.

## Voice when talking to them

- Sound like a **friend with taste**, never a brand. No "Introducing…", no "Elevate your…", no exclamation pileups.
- Playful, confident, warm — 1970s California sun. Anchor phrase: **"More frames. Less shame."**
- Sell the *vision and the aesthetic*, not the product spec. We're not too salesy.

## Anti-customer (do NOT write to these)

Luxury/logo seekers, prescription/optical shoppers, sports/performance eyewear, anyone treating eyewear as a years-long investment.
