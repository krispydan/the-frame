# Jaxy Content Pillars (video)

*The recurring themes our short-form videos rotate through. A feed built on
pillars reads as intentional; random clips read as noise. Every video should
sit in ONE pillar and lead with an angle that fits it. Edit these as we learn
what performs.*

## The pillars

### 1. Fit Check / Real Faces
Frames on real, varied people in real light and motion — the #1 buying trigger is *seeing someone cool wearing them*. Angle: "which one's your face?", styling on different vibes, before/after a frame swap.
- Hook energy: *"POV: you finally found your shape."*

### 2. The Shape Hero
One distinctive frame, front and center — the design doing the talking. Close, tight, punchy. Answers "will they look cheap?" by letting detail argue for itself.
- Hook energy: *"This shape has no business being $25."*

### 3. Two of These, Please (Collect the set)
Multiple pairs, colorways, moods — glasses as an accessory you own several of, matched to outfits/seasons. Drives multi-unit, guilt-free impulse.
- Hook energy: *"One pair? In this economy? Absolutely not."*

### 4. Myth / Hot Take / Objection
Bust the "cheap = junk" belief, the "designer or bust" belief, the "these won't suit me" belief. Education disguised as opinion — built for shares and comments.
- Hook energy: *"Stop paying $150 for sunglasses. Here's why."*

### 5. Behind Jaxy / Community
Origin, the LA sun aesthetic, the "glasses for everyone we know" family energy, boutiques and customers in the wild. Builds trust and belonging.
- Hook energy: *"Born in LA. Built for everywhere."*

## How pillars steer a video

- Pick the pillar that fits the clips, then write the **hook to that pillar's energy**.
- Rotate pillars across the week so the feed doesn't repeat itself.
- Sales/product pushes live inside pillars 1–4 (education-first), never as bare ads.

## Anchor

Everything ladders back to **"More frames. Less shame."** — accessible, distinctive, guilt-free.
